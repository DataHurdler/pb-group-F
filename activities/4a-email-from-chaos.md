# Activity 4a: Email from Chaos

Block 4 · Outcome: LO3 · Time: 3 + 22 min · Format: volunteer reading, then pairs

## For the instructor

**Why this activity:** a confusing email is a universal federal experience, and
turning one into a clear reply exercises everything: find the real questions,
answer each, flag what you don't know, commit to nothing you can't deliver. The
first draft is almost never right, which makes it ideal for practising
iteration.

**Setup:** pick a volunteer who looks game, and give them the email two minutes
early. Print the learner section for everyone.

**Scoring** (per pair, from their final reply)

| | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Each of the five asks answered clearly | +1 (max 5) |
| Deadline flagged as unconfirmed, not stated as fact | +2 |
| No invented commitments, names, emails, or dates | +2 |
| Log shows three specific follow-ups | +1 |

**Answer key: the five asks hidden in the email**

1. When can you send the FY26 Q3 numbers? → **Thursday, October 29**
2. Old template or new? → **New template (v2.1)**
3. Who's the POC now that Lisa has moved? → **Dana Kim**
4. Call on Tuesday or Wednesday? → **Tuesday, 14:00–15:00 only**
5. Has the deadline moved to the 30th? → **Unknown: [CHECK] with the
   report coordinator; don't confirm**

**Common first-draft failures:** answers three or four asks, not five; commits to
"Friday or Monday" because the email suggested it; confirms the 30th as the
deadline; invents an email address for Dana Kim; runs to 250 words; copies the
sender's acronyms.

**Moves that usually fix them:** "List every question in the email first";
"Answer each, numbered, in one sentence"; "Use only the facts I gave you; write
[CHECK] for anything else"; "Under 120 words."

## For learners

### The email

All names and details are fabricated.

> **From:** T. Hargrove
> **Subject:** RE: RE: FW: RE: Q3 numbers?? + call
>
> Hi! Hope your week's going OK, ours has been NUTS with the CJ stuff (as I'm
> sure you've heard lol). So per my last few emails and also what Marcus said on
> the thing Tuesday, we really need the FY26 Q3 numbers for the congressional
> report whenever you can, Friday is ideal but honestly Monday's fine too unless
> it isn't, I'll let you know. Also are you guys doing the new template or the
> old one because I've been getting both and the columns don't match which is a
> whole thing. Separately, since Lisa moved over to OCFO (congrats to her!!) I'm
> not sure who the POC is on your side now? Could we maybe get 30 min on the
> calendar, Tues or Weds works for me, or Thurs AM, actually not Thurs. Oh and I
> heard the deadline for the whole report got moved to the 30th but don't quote
> me on that. Anyway let me know!! Thx!!!
> — T

### What you know (the facts for your reply)

- Q3 numbers will be ready **Thursday, October 29**. Not before.
- Your team is using the **new template, version 2.1**.
- The new POC is **Dana Kim**.
- You're free for a call **Tuesday 14:00–15:00 only**.
- You **don't know** whether the report deadline has moved.

### Your task

Use ChatGPT to write a reply that answers every question, flags what you don't
know, and commits to nothing you can't deliver. Do at least three rounds, each a
**specific follow-up**, not a fresh start.

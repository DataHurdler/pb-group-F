# Activity 2b: Two Truths and an AI

Block 2 · Outcomes: LO1, LO4 · Time: 6 min · Format: teams, A/B/C cards

## For the instructor

**Why this game:** a fast-paced follow-up to the Hunt. Each round shows a short
source and three statements "from ChatGPT's summary." Two are supported; one is
a plausible fabrication. The lesson: the fabrication is usually the most
reasonable-sounding statement, because it's what you'd *expect* the source to
say.

**Run it:** show the source and statements on screen. Two minutes per round:
60 seconds to decide; on "3, 2, 1," every team holds up a card; one point per
correct team; then ask one team how they knew.

**Answers:** round 1, **C** · round 2, **C** · round 3, **C**. (Shuffle the
letters on your slides so C isn't always the answer.)

---

## Rounds

All content is fabricated.

### Round 1: meeting notes

> *Source:* The data-quality working group met October 14. It agreed to meet
> monthly. Co-chairs: R. Okafor and T. Lin. Charter draft due November 4.

- A. The group will meet monthly.
- B. The charter draft is due November 4.
- C. The co-chairs will rotate annually.

### Round 2: a data table

> *Source:* Data requests received: FY2024, 3,100; FY2025, 3,550; FY2026, 3,480.

- A. Requests rose from FY2024 to FY2025.
- B. Requests fell slightly in FY2026.
- C. Requests have grown every year since FY2024.

### Round 3: a policy excerpt

> *Source:* Staff may use approved AI tools to draft internal documents.
> Outputs must be reviewed by the author before sharing. AI tools may not be
> used to make final decisions on personnel actions.

- A. Authors must review AI outputs before sharing them.
- B. AI tools may not make final decisions on personnel actions.
- C. AI-drafted documents must be labelled as AI-generated.

Round 3's debrief: C sounds like a rule an agency *might* have, which is exactly
why it's convincing. Whether your agency requires labelling is a real question
for its policy, not for ChatGPT.

# Activity 1a: Human or Machine?

Block 1 · Outcome: LO1 · Time: 7 min · Format: teams, A/B cards

## For the instructor

**Why this game:** an opener that gets every table talking within five minutes,
and plants the day's first lesson without a slide: a vague prompt gives a
polished, generic answer.

**Prep (the day before):** in the learners' version of ChatGPT, start a new chat
and send exactly: *"Write an agenda for a quarterly data review meeting."* Paste
the output into the handout as one of the two agendas. Flip a coin for whether
it's A or B, and keep the human one below as the other. Record which is which.

**Timing**

| Min | Step |
| --- | --- |
| 0–1 | "One of these was written by a person, one by ChatGPT. Which is the machine?" |
| 1–4 | Teams read and decide; one card up per team on "3, 2, 1" |
| 4–7 | Reveal. Points to correct teams. Ask: *what gave it away?* |

**What usually gives it away:** the ChatGPT agenda is generic ("Welcome and
introductions," "Review of key metrics," "Open discussion"), evenly timed, and
has no names, decisions, or real problems. The human agenda is uneven and
specific: names, an open dispute, a decision needed.

**The line to end on:** *"It wasn't hard to spot, because ChatGPT only had nine
words to work with. By lunch you'll write prompts where it's much harder to
tell."*

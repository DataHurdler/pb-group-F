# Activity 1.2: Check the memo

Session: 01 · Outcomes: LO1, LO4 · Time: 18 min · Format: tables of 3–4

## For the instructor

**Why this activity:** finding fabrications by reading against a source is the
single most important habit in the course. This is the first practice.

**Setup:** print the learner section (source facts and memo), one per learner.
No tool is needed for this one.

**Timing**

| Min | Step |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 | Brief: the facts are what the drafter gave the AI. The memo is what came back. Find every problem |
| 2–14 | Tables work |
| 14–18 | Go through the answer key; ask tables which they missed |

**Answer key: six planted problems**

| # | In the memo | Problem | How you'd catch it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | "in accordance with 2 CFR 200.329(c)(4)" | Not in the source. It looks authoritative, but the drafter never supplied it. It could be invented, or real but not say this | Look it up in eCFR, or delete it |
| 2 | "30 business days" | Source says **calendar** days | Compare line by line |
| 3 | "down from 60 days" | Source says the old deadline was **45** days | Compare line by line |
| 4 | "due January 15, 2027" | Q1 FY2027 ends December 31, 2026. Thirty calendar days later is **January 30, 2027** | Do the date math yourself |
| 5 | "improve compliance by 37 percent" | Invented statistic; no source | Delete, or find a real source |
| 6 | "will have their funding suspended" | Source says late reports are **flagged** in the system. The memo invents a penalty | Compare line by line |

Also worth noting if a table raises it: "Effective immediately" is a softer
mismatch. The source says the change starts with Q1 FY2027.

**If a table finishes early:** rewrite the prompt the drafter should have used,
so that none of these six problems would have appeared.

**If a table is stuck:** "Put a finger on each sentence of the memo and find the
line in the facts that supports it."

## For learners

**Your task:** a colleague gave an AI tool the facts below and asked for a memo
to grant recipients. Find every place where the memo doesn't match the facts or
adds something the facts don't support.

All names and details are fabricated.

### The facts the AI was given

- Starting with Q1 of FY2027, quarterly progress reports are due 30 calendar days
  after the end of each quarter.
- The current deadline is 45 days.
- Applies to all active grants managed by the Grants Coordination Office.
- Late reports will be flagged in the grants management system.
- Questions: Dana Ortiz, ext. 4410.

### The memo it produced

> **MEMORANDUM FOR:** All Grant Recipients
> **FROM:** Grants Coordination Office
> **SUBJECT:** Updated Quarterly Reporting Deadline
>
> Effective immediately, and in accordance with 2 CFR 200.329(c)(4), quarterly
> progress reports are due 30 business days after the end of each quarter, down
> from 60 days. For the first quarter of FY2027, which ends December 31, 2026,
> reports are therefore due January 15, 2027.
>
> Research shows that shorter reporting cycles improve compliance by 37 percent.
> Recipients who submit late will have their funding suspended.
>
> Please direct questions to Dana Ortiz at extension 4410.

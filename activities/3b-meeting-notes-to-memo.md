# Scenario B: Meeting notes to a BLUF memo

Session: 03 · Outcomes: LO2, LO3, LO4 · Time: 23 min · Format: tables of 3–4

## For the instructor

**Why this activity:** real source material is messy and contains things that
shouldn't travel. Learners have to read before they paste, decide what belongs
in the output, and catch the tool inventing a decision. No checklist this time.

**Setup:** handout on every desk. One device per table.

**Timing**

| Min | Step |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 | Brief: read the notes **before** you paste anything |
| 2–20 | Tables work |
| 20–23 | Each table writes one sentence: what did you remove, and why? |

**What a strong response does**

- **Medical detail:** removed before pasting, and absent from the memo. The memo
  needs only "K. Williams will be out from October 12 for about six weeks; her 14
  grants are being split between M. Chen and J. Brooks." The reason for leave is
  personal medical information and doesn't belong in either the tool or the memo.
- **Budget figure:** a deliberate decision, stated. Strong tables either leave it
  out of the paste (it's pre-decisional and marked "don't share") and note
  verbally to the Deputy Director that there's a budget item to discuss, or
  include it only if the tool is approved for that information. Either is
  defensible; *not noticing* is the miss.
- **Open decision stays open:** whether to delay the reminder email was **not
  decided**. Many outputs state a decision. The memo should list it as a decision
  needed by Friday, October 9.
- **Unassigned item stays unassigned:** the FAQ owner wasn't named. Watch for the
  tool assigning it to someone.
- **BLUF first,** under 250 words, owners and dates on every action.

**Trade-off to raise in the debrief:** brevity against completeness. A
Deputy Director needs the slipped go-live date and the open decision; they may
not need the training logistics.

**If a table finishes early:** ask the tool to list every decision in the notes
and say whether each was made or still open. Was it right?

## For learners

**Your task:** Deputy Director Alvarez missed this meeting. Use the AI tool to
turn the notes into a BLUF memo for her: under 250 words, bottom line first,
then decisions needed from her, action items with owners and dates, and risks.

**Read the notes before you paste anything.** Decide what should and shouldn't
go into the tool, and into the memo.

All names, dates, and details are fabricated.

> **Weekly program sync, Tuesday, October 6**
> Attendees: R. Patel (chair), D. Ortiz, M. Chen, K. Williams, J. Brooks
>
> - **Portal migration:** vendor says go-live is slipping from Nov 2 to Nov 16.
>   Patel needs a decision by Fri Oct 9 on whether to delay the Q1 reporting
>   reminder email. Chen suggests keeping the Nov 2 email but pointing to the old
>   portal. No decision made.
> - **Q1 reporting deadline change (45 to 30 days):** comms memo drafted. Ortiz to
>   send to all recipients by Oct 9.
> - **Staffing:** K. Williams out on medical leave from Oct 12 for about six weeks
>   (knee surgery). Her portfolio (14 grants) to be split between Chen and Brooks.
> - **Budget:** Patel said FY27 travel will likely be cut 20 percent. "Not final,
>   don't share outside this room."
> - **Training:** Brooks to schedule two portal training sessions for grantees,
>   week of Nov 16, depending on go-live.
> - **FAQ update:** needs an owner. Not assigned.

**When you're done, write one sentence:** what did you remove, and why?

**Time:** 20 minutes

# Activity 6: Your prompt card

Block 6 · Outcomes: LO2, LO3, LO4 · Time: 38 min, plus gallery walk · Format: individual, partner test

## For the instructor

**Why this activity:** it's what learners take back to their desks, and on one
page it shows whether each outcome landed.

**Setup:** print the learner section, one per learner. Sticky notes back to their
owners from the goals wall. Three sticky dots per learner for the gallery walk.

**What good looks like:** a colleague could use the card without asking the
author anything; placeholders mark everything that changes; a [CHECK]
guardrail; a specific checking list; a clear "never paste" line.

**Likely mistakes:**
- A one-off prompt with no placeholders. Ask: what would you change next time?
- A checking list that says "check it". Ask: check what, against what?
- A real document as test input. Stop them, kindly, and help them invent one.

**If someone is stuck for a task:** suggest one of the day's three (agenda,
checklist, email reply), adapted to their own office, or a briefing memo from
notes.

### Rubric

| | Criterion | Meets | Not yet |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **All five parts**, specific to the task (LO2) | Each would change the output if removed | Missing or generic |
| 2 | **Reusable:** placeholders for everything that changes (LO2) | A colleague could use it without asking you | Works only on the test input |
| 3 | **Safe and checked:** specific checking list and "never paste" line (LO4) | Names which facts, against what | "Check it" or blank |
| 4 | **Improved** after testing, with a ready follow-up (LO3) | One concrete change, and why | No change |

The rubric scores the card, not how good ChatGPT's output was.

# Scenario A: Plain-language rewrite

Session: 03 · Outcomes: LO3, LO4 · Time: 25 min · Format: pairs

## For the instructor

**Why this activity:** plain-language rewriting is one of the most useful federal
applications, and one of the riskiest: the tool readily simplifies a requirement
until it's wrong. Learners practise iterating *and* checking.

**Setup:** handout on every desk. Pairs share a device.

**Timing**

| Min | Step |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 | Brief: at least three rounds; log every round |
| 2–22 | Pairs work |
| 22–25 | Each pair checks its final version against the requirement list |

**Answer key: the eight requirements a correct rewrite keeps**

1. Report due **30 calendar days** after each federal fiscal quarter ends.
2. Sent to the Grants Coordination Office.
3. Includes a narrative of activities.
4. Includes measures of progress toward approved objectives.
5. Includes an itemised list of expenses for the period.
6. Extension requests must be **in writing**, at least **10 business days**
   before the due date.
7. Extensions are **at most 15 calendar days**, at the Grants Officer's
   **discretion** (not guaranteed).
8. Late reports without an approved extension **may** lead to payments being
   **withheld** until the report is accepted.

**Most common losses:** "calendar" dropped; "may" becoming "will"; "at the
Grants Officer's discretion" dropped, so extensions sound automatic; the three
report contents merged into "a progress report."

**Moves that usually fix them:** "First list every requirement, then rewrite";
"keep all numbers, deadlines, and conditions exactly"; a follow-up pointing at
the specific loss.

**If a pair finishes early:** ask the tool to list every change it made from the
source, then check whether its list is complete.

## For learners

**Your task:** rewrite the paragraph below for owners of small businesses that
receive grants. Plain language, roughly an 8th-grade reading level, and **no
requirement lost or changed**. Do at least three rounds, and log each one.

The paragraph is fabricated.

> Pursuant to the terms and conditions of the award, recipients shall, no later
> than 30 calendar days following the conclusion of each federal fiscal quarter,
> submit to the cognizant Grants Coordination Office a performance progress
> report, inclusive of a narrative description of activities undertaken,
> quantitative measures of progress toward approved objectives, and an itemized
> accounting of expenditures incurred during the reporting period. Recipients who
> anticipate an inability to meet the aforementioned deadline must submit a
> written request for an extension not fewer than 10 business days prior to the
> due date; extensions shall not exceed 15 calendar days and shall be granted at
> the sole discretion of the Grants Officer. Failure to submit a timely report
> absent an approved extension may result in the withholding of subsequent
> payment requests until such time as the delinquent report has been received and
> accepted.

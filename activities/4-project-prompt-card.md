# Project: A prompt card for your own task

Session: 04 · Outcomes: LO2, LO3, LO4 · Time: 55 min · Format: individual, with a partner test

## For the instructor

**Why this activity:** it's the one thing learners take back to their desk. It
also shows you, on one page, whether each outcome landed.

**Setup:** print the learner section (brief, card, and rubric), one per learner.
Sticky notes from the goals wall back to their owners.

**Timing:** see [../sessions/04-project.md](../sessions/04-project.md).

**What good looks like:** a card a colleague could pick up and use without asking
the author anything; placeholders for everything that changes each time; a
[CHECK] guardrail; a specific verification list; a clear "never paste" line.

**Likely mistakes:**
- The template is really a one-off prompt with no placeholders. Ask: what would
  you change next time?
- The verification list says "check it". Ask: check what, against what?
- The test input is a real document. Stop and help them invent one.

**If a learner finishes early:** run the card in a fresh conversation, then write a
second card for a different task.

**If a learner is stuck choosing a task:** routine writing works best: replies to
common inquiries, meeting summaries, plain-language rewrites, first drafts of
recurring reports, checklists from procedures.

## For learners

**Your task:** build a prompt card for a task you do regularly at work (the one on
your sticky note, or another). Test it on **invented** input, have a partner
test it, and improve it once.

Never use a real document as test input today. A realistic fake works just as
well.

### Rubric

Score your own card. Your partner can score it too.

| | Criterion | Meets | Not yet |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **All five parts** are present and specific to the task (LO2) | Each part would change the output if removed | One or more parts missing or generic |
| 2 | **Reusable:** placeholders mark everything that changes (LO2) | A colleague could use it without asking you anything | Works only for the test input |
| 3 | **Safe and checked:** a specific verification list and a "never paste" line (LO4) | Names which facts to check, against what | "Check it" or blank |
| 4 | **Tested and improved** after the partner test (LO3) | One concrete change, and why | No change, or the change isn't explained |

The rubric scores the prompt card, not how good the AI's output was.

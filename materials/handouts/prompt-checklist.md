# ChatGPT prompt checklist

One page. Keep it next to your keyboard.

## The five parts

| Part | Ask yourself | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Task** | What exactly do I want? | "Draft a reply to the email below" |
| **Context** | What facts can't it know? Paste them | "Q3 numbers ready Oct 29. New POC: Dana Kim. [email pasted]" |
| **Audience** | Who uses this, and what do they care about? | "A busy counterpart in another office who's been getting mixed messages" |
| **Format** | Length, structure, tone? | "Under 120 words. Answer each question, numbered. Friendly, professional" |
| **Guardrails** | What must it not do? What if it's unsure? | "Use only these facts. Don't commit to dates I haven't given. Write [CHECK] for anything missing" |

## Starters for the three tasks

- **Meeting agenda:** "Draft a [length] agenda for [meeting] with [attendees].
  Goals: [goals]. Open items from last time: [items]. Timed items that add up to
  [length], an owner for each, decisions needed in bold, and an action-item table
  (action, owner, due date) at the end. Use only these facts; [CHECK] for gaps."
- **Implementation checklist:** "First list the phases of [project]. Then, for
  each phase, list tasks with owner, due date, and dependency. Then give me a
  checklist table. Use only the facts below; [CHECK] for any missing owner or
  date. [summary]"
- **Confusing email:** "List every question this email asks. Then draft a reply
  that answers each one in a sentence, numbered, under [N] words, using only
  these facts: [facts]. Flag anything I haven't confirmed. [email]"

## When the first answer isn't right

1. **Point at the problem:** "You missed the question about the template."
2. **Show an example** of the tone or format you want.
3. **Ask it to ask you** up to five questions first.
4. **Break it into steps:** list, then draft, then check.
5. **Ask what it changed or assumed**, then check that yourself.

**Edit your earlier message** to rewrite from that point; **add a follow-up** to
keep the history. **Start a new chat** when it keeps repeating a mistake or you
switch tasks.

## Before you use the output

- [ ] Every number, name, and date matches the source
- [ ] Every source or citation looked up by you
- [ ] No claims of cause the evidence doesn't support
- [ ] No commitments you haven't agreed to
- [ ] Every question answered and every [CHECK] resolved
- [ ] You'd sign it, because you are

## Before you paste

If it isn't needed for the task, it doesn't go in. See the safe-use card.

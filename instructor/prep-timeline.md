# Preparation timeline

## Four weeks out

- [ ] Open questions in [../BRIEF.md](../BRIEF.md), section 1, answered,
      **especially the tool**
- [ ] Everything in [federal-context.md](federal-context.md) confirmed with the
      host agency
- [ ] Safe-use card updated with the agency's policy link and contact
- [ ] Venue booked; tables, not rows; network access to the tool confirmed
- [ ] Ask the host whether any learners need accommodations

## Three weeks out

- [ ] **Run every activity yourself in the tool learners will use.** Note where
      your outputs differ from the examples in the activity files, and adjust
      the instructor notes
- [ ] Pre/post check questions reviewed against the final outcomes
- [ ] Slides built from the session outlines

## Two weeks out

- [ ] Dry run of activities 1.2, 2.1, and scenario A on a colleague, timed.
      If one runs long, cut it now
- [ ] Pre-course email sent: [../learners/pre-course-email.md](../learners/pre-course-email.md)

## One week out

- [ ] Full rehearsal of the morning, out loud, with a timer and the tool
- [ ] Final numbers; printing ordered (list in [../materials/README.md](../materials/README.md))
- [ ] Learners who report access problems helped before the day

## Day before

- [ ] Reminder email sent
- [ ] Printouts sorted by session
- [ ] Slides on your laptop and in a second place
- [ ] Re-run the Session 02 demo; tools change, and so do their answers
- [ ] Run sheet printed: [run-sheet.md](run-sheet.md)

## Day of

Follow [run-sheet.md](run-sheet.md). Arrive 45 minutes early.

## After

- [ ] Within 48 hours: follow-up email, with parking-lot answers and the agency
      policy link
- [ ] Within a week: feedback and pre/post results summarised; changes written
      into this repo

# Federal context: confirm before teaching

Federal AI policy changes quickly and differs between agencies. This course
teaches general safe practice and points learners to their agency's rules. It
doesn't quote policy as current. Confirm each item below with the host agency at
least four weeks out, and update the safe-use card to match.

## Government-wide policy to check is still current

As this course was written, government-wide direction on federal AI use came
from OMB memoranda (M-25-21 on federal use of AI, and M-25-22 on AI acquisition,
both April 2025). **Check whether these are still current before quoting them,**
and prefer the host agency's own policy, which implements them. This course
doesn't need learners to know memo numbers. It needs them to know their
agency's rules and where to find them.

Other rules that bear on what learners paste, which you can mention without
detail: the Privacy Act, the CUI program, the Federal Records Act, and Section
508 for anything published.

## Questions you'll get, and answers that keep you in your lane

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| "Can I use ChatGPT on my phone for work?" | Depends on agency policy; most restrict work information to approved tools. Ask your supervisor; don't take my word for your agency |
| "Can I use it to write performance reviews / evaluate applicants?" | Decisions about people carry the highest risk, and many agencies restrict them. Check policy first. The safe-use card lists this under "stop and ask" |
| "Is what I type private?" | Depends on the tool's terms and your agency's agreement. Assume it isn't unless your agency has told you otherwise |
| "Is it allowed to draft responses to the public?" | Often yes with an approved tool and human review. The output is still yours: check it |
| "Will this replace my job?" | Not your call or mine today. What's clear is that people who can use it well and check it carefully are more useful, not less |
| "Which tool is best?" | The one your agency approves. The techniques today work in any of them |

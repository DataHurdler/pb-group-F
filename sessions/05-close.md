# 05 Close

Status: draft
Time: 15:15–15:45 (30 min) · Talk 15 / do 15
Outcomes: all

## Purpose

Learners see how far they've come, commit to one next step they're actually
allowed to take, and tell you what to change.

## Outline

| Min | Segment | Format | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6 | Review | Room | Back to the goals wall. Which tasks did the prompt cards cover? Which goals weren't met, and where can those people go next? |
| 6–10 | Parking lot | Instructor | Answer what you can in a sentence each. Promise written answers to the rest in the follow-up email |
| 10–16 | Post-check | Individual | Same five questions as the morning. If you can score it quickly, show the room how it moved |
| 16–24 | Action plan | Individual, then pairs | On the agenda handout: **the task I'll use my prompt card on, when, and what I'll check first** (agency policy, the tool I'm allowed to use, whether the data is permitted). Tell a partner |
| 24–28 | Feedback | Individual | [../assessment/feedback-form.md](../assessment/feedback-form.md) |
| 28–30 | Thanks | Instructor | Where to go next (below). What's coming in the follow-up email |

## Last thing to say

*Before you use any of this on real work, check two things: is this tool
approved for this information, and who will check the output? Then use your
card.*

## Notes

**Never cut this session.** If short of time, the review becomes one question
(cut 4), but keep the post-check, action plan, and feedback. Learners who must
leave early do the feedback form first.

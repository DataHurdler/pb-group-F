# Block 6: Your prompt card, and close

Status: draft
Time: 15:30–16:30 (60 min) · Talk 10 / do 50
Outcomes: all

## Purpose

Each learner leaves with a tested, reusable ChatGPT prompt for a task from their
own job, and a plan to use it safely.

## Outline

| Min | Segment | Format | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 | Brief | Instructor | [The prompt card](../activities/6-prompt-card.md) and rubric. One rule: **invent the test input** |
| 3–25 | Build and test | Individual | Take your sticky note from the goals wall (or pick an agenda, checklist, or email task). Fill in the card, write an invented test input, run it, improve it |
| 25–33 | Partner test | Pairs | Swap. Your partner fills your placeholders with *their* invented input and runs it, then tells you one thing that broke. Fix it |
| 33–41 | **Gallery walk** | Room | Cards on the wall. Everyone has three sticky dots: put them on the prompts you'd steal for your own work. Read the prompts, not the outputs |
| 41–47 | Post-check | Individual | Same five questions as 8:56 |
| 47–53 | Action plan and feedback | Individual | On the agenda handout: *the task I'll use my card on, when, and what I'll check first.* Then the [feedback form](../assessment/feedback-form.md) |
| 53–60 | Close | Instructor | Final scoreboard and nominal prizes. Show how the pre/post check moved, if you've had time to tally. Where to go next. The last line, below |

## The last thing to say

*Before you use your card on real work, check two things: is this version of
ChatGPT approved for this information, and who will check the output? Then use
it on Monday.*

## Notes

- **Stop the build on time.** An unfinished card still goes home.
- If a learner's task can't be tested with invented input, help them pick a
  nearby task that can. That choice is itself a LO4 judgement.
- If your version of ChatGPT supports custom instructions, Projects, or custom
  GPTs, and the agency allows them, show fast finishers how to save their card
  there. It's optional; the card works by copy and paste in any version.
- **Never cut the action plan.** If you're behind, use cut 4 (gallery walk
  becomes "hold up your card").

# Block 6: Your prompt card, and close

Status: draft
Time: 15:30–16:30 (60 min) · Talk 12 / do 48
Outcomes: all

## Purpose

Each learner leaves with a reusable ChatGPT prompt for a task from their own
job, **tested** on inputs designed to break it, and a plan to use it safely.

## Outline

| Min | Segment | Format | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Brief: how to test a prompt | Instructor | [The prompt card](../activities/6-prompt-card.md) and rubric, then the six testing steps (below). One rule: **invent the test input** |
| 5–25 | Build and test | Individual | Take your sticky note from the goals wall (or pick an agenda, checklist, or email task). Write pass criteria first, then the card. Run it on the **normal** and **missing-information** inputs from the nearest [test pack](../prompts/block-6.md), or your own. Log results; change one thing at a time |
| 25–33 | Partner test | Pairs | Swap. Your partner runs your card on the **messy** input from the pack, or their own invented one, and scores it against *your* pass criteria. Fix what broke |
| 33–41 | **Gallery walk** | Room | Cards on the wall. Everyone has three sticky dots for the prompts they'd steal for their own work. Read the prompts and test logs, not the outputs |
| 41–47 | Post-check | Individual | Same five questions as 8:56 |
| 47–53 | Action plan and feedback | Individual | On the agenda handout: *the task I'll use my card on, when, and what I'll check first.* Point to the [take-home practice](../prompts/take-home.md). Then the [feedback form](../assessment/feedback-form.md) |
| 53–60 | Close | Instructor | Final scoreboard and nominal prizes. How the pre/post check moved, if tallied. Where to go next. The last line, below |

## How to test a prompt (the 3-minute version)

From [../prompts/README.md](../prompts/README.md). Learners already did each
step informally in Blocks 3–5; now they get the whole method.

1. Write pass criteria **before** running: three to five things someone else
   could check.
2. Normal input. 3. Messy input. 4. Missing-information input: [CHECK] or
   invention? 5. Twice in new chats. 6. Change one thing at a time.

*"You've tested every prompt today. In Block 3 you swapped cards, in Block 4 you
tried a different email, in Block 5 you traced every date. This is the same thing,
written down so you can do it on Monday."*

## The last thing to say

*Today you used the public version of ChatGPT with invented information. Before
you use your card on real work, check two things: is there a tool your agency
has approved for this information, and who will check the output?*

## Notes

- **Stop the build on time.** An unfinished card still goes home.
- If a learner's task can't be tested with invented input, help them pick a
  nearby task that can. That choice is itself a LO4 judgement.
- Fast finishers: run the card twice in new chats (testing step 5), or save it in
  ChatGPT's custom instructions, if that's allowed and available.
- **Never cut the build-and-test time or the action plan.** If you're behind, use
  cut 4 (gallery walk becomes "hold up your card").

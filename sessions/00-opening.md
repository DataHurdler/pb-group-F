# 00 Opening and safe-use rules

Status: draft
Time: 9:00–9:30 (30 min) · Talk 12 / do 18
Outcomes: sets up the day; introduces LO4

## Purpose

Learners know what the day is for, know what's safe to type before they type
anything, and have a working tool. You learn what they already know.

## Outline

| Min | Segment | Format | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4 | Welcome | Instructor | Who you are, in two sentences. Wi-Fi, restrooms, break times, the 15:45 finish. Point to the parking-lot sheet |
| 4–12 | Introductions | Tables, then room | At tables: name, office, and **one recurring task you'd like help with**, written on a sticky note without any sensitive detail. Notes go on the goals wall. Read a few aloud. Tell them: this note becomes your afternoon project |
| 12–18 | The day | Instructor | The four outcomes in plain words. What's out of scope, and where to send those questions (see [../BRIEF.md](../BRIEF.md), section 5) |
| 18–23 | **Safe-use rules** | Instructor | Walk through [../materials/handouts/safe-use-card.md](../materials/handouts/safe-use-card.md). Then say the room rule plainly: *today, everything you paste is fabricated or public. Nothing from your real work goes in* |
| 23–28 | Pre-check | Individual | [../assessment/pre-post-check.md](../assessment/pre-post-check.md). Anonymous, not graded |
| 28–30 | Setup check | Individual | Everyone opens the tool and sends: *"Reply with the word ready."* Hands up if it didn't work |

## What to say about the worries in the room

Answer these before anyone asks. It lowers the temperature for the whole day.

- **"Am I allowed to use this?"** It depends on your agency's policy and the tool.
  Today we use [TODO: the tool], with invented data only, so everything you do
  in this room is fine. Before using it on real work, check your agency's AI
  policy and ask your supervisor.
- **"Will it replace my job?"** Today is about using a tool well, and the tool
  doesn't know your programs, your stakeholders, or your judgement. You'll see
  exactly where it falls short by 10:30.
- **"Can I trust it?"** Not without checking. That's most of Session 01.

## Materials

- Goals wall (flip chart) and sticky notes; parking-lot sheet
- Safe-use card, printed, one per learner
- Agenda handout: [../learners/agenda-handout.md](../learners/agenda-handout.md)
- Pre-check, printed or as a link

## Check before moving on

Every learner has a sticky note on the wall and a tool that answered "ready".
Anyone whose tool failed is paired with a neighbour now; fix it at the break.

## Notes

- Never cut the safe-use rules, even if you start late.
- Keep the goals wall up all day. You'll return to it at 15:15 and learners
  will use their notes at 14:15.

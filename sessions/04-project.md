# 04 Project: a prompt you'll reuse

Status: draft
Time: 14:15–15:15 (60 min) · Talk 8 / do 52
Outcomes: LO2, LO3, LO4

## Purpose

Each learner leaves with something they'll actually use: a tested, reusable
prompt for a recurring task from their own job, with its safety notes attached.

## By the end of this session, learners can…

- Turn a one-off prompt into a reusable template with placeholders (LO2).
- Test it, find a weakness, and fix it (LO3).
- Write down what must be checked and what must never go in (LO4).

## The task

The full brief, card template, and rubric are in
[../activities/4-project-prompt-card.md](../activities/4-project-prompt-card.md).
In short: take the task from your sticky note on the goals wall, build a prompt
card for it, test it on **invented** input, have a partner test it on their own
invented input, and improve it once.

## Outline

| Min | Segment | Format | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Brief | Instructor | The prompt card and rubric, on paper. One rule: **invent the test input**. A realistic fake is better than a real document |
| 5–25 | Build and test | Individual | Fill in the card, write a fabricated test input, run it, adjust. Visit every learner by minute 20 |
| 25–35 | Partner test | Pairs | Swap cards. Your partner fills the placeholders with their own invented input and runs it, then tells you one thing that broke or confused them |
| 35–45 | Revise | Individual | Fix the weakness your partner found. Score your card against the rubric |
| 45–57 | Share-outs | Room | 4–5 volunteers, 2 minutes each: **read the prompt, not the output**, and say what the partner test changed |
| 57–60 | Bridge | Instructor | Into the close |

## Why "read the prompt, not the output"

Outputs turn share-outs into a talent show and are hard to judge from the back
of the room. Prompts are the transferable thing. Hold the rule even when someone
is proud of what came out.

## Materials

- Project brief, prompt card, and rubric, printed, one per learner
- Learners' sticky notes from the goals wall

## Notes

- **Stop on time.** An unfinished card is still worth taking home.
- If a learner's task can't be done safely with invented input (it depends on a
  real case file, for instance), help them choose a nearby task that can, and
  point out that this is itself a LO4 judgement.
- Learners who finish early: run the card in a fresh conversation to make sure
  it works without anything from earlier in the chat.
- If you're behind: cut 3 in [../schedule.md](../schedule.md), two volunteers only.
  **Never cut the work time.**

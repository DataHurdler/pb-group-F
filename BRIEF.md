## 2. Assumptions

- **Six hours of contact time**, not counting lunch: 9:00–15:45, with a 45-minute
  lunch and two 15-minute breaks, leaving 330 minutes of teaching.
- **In person**, one instructor, 12–24 learners at tables of three or four.
- **Each learner has access to one general-purpose chat assistant** (for example,
  an agency-provided enterprise tool). Activities use only fabricated or public
  text, so they're safe in any tool the learner is permitted to use.
- **No formal assessment.** The pre/post check and project are for learning and
  for improving the course.

## 4. Audience

- **Who:** US federal employees in non-technical roles: program and policy
  staff, analysts, administrative and HR professionals, grants and contract
  specialists, communications staff, and supervisors. Any grade.
- **Already know:** their own work well; federal writing conventions (memos,
  BLUF, correspondence); basic computer use. Many will have tried a consumer AI
  chatbot casually.
- **Don't assume they know:** how LLMs work; any structured way of prompting;
  their agency's AI policy; which data rules apply to AI tools.
- **Why they're coming:** a mix. Some are told to; some want to keep up; some
  have seen colleagues save time.
- **Likely worries:** *Am I allowed to use this? Will I get in trouble? Will it
  replace my job? Can I trust what it says?* The course answers the first three
  directly in Session 00 and the last throughout.

## 5. Scope

**In:** how LLMs generate text; writing, refining, and reusing prompts for
everyday knowledge work; checking outputs; data-handling basics for AI tools;
accountability and disclosure.

**Out, and where to send people who ask:**

| Topic | Send them to |
| --- | --- |
| Building, buying, or deploying AI systems | The agency's Chief AI Officer or CIO office |
| AI acquisition and contracting rules | The agency's acquisition policy office |
| AI risk management and governance | The agency's AI governance body |
| Coding with AI, agents, image generation | A follow-on course, if one exists [TODO] |
| Classified environments | The agency's security office |
| Whether a specific real task is allowed | Their supervisor and agency AI policy, not the instructor |

## 2. Decided

- **Six hours of instruction**, plus a **one-hour lunch** and **four 15-minute
  breaks** (two in the morning, two in the afternoon): 8:30–16:30.
- **Tool: ChatGPT.** Techniques are taught so they work on any version; features
  that only some versions have (custom instructions, Projects, custom GPTs, file
  upload) are optional extras.
- **Target tasks:** a meeting agenda with action items, a project implementation
  checklist, and turning a confusing email into a clear response. Research,
  analysis, report writing, and briefings supply the context and examples.
- **Out:** grant writing and medical information. Neither appears in any
  example.

## 3. Assumptions

- In person, one instructor, 12–24 learners in teams of 3–4.
- No formal assessment; the pre/post check and prompt cards are for learning and
  for improving the course.

## 5. Audience

- **Who:** US federal employees with at least a bachelor's degree, in roles
  centred on research, analysis, report writing, and briefings: policy and
  program analysts, researchers, evaluators, and their supervisors.
- **Already know:** their subject matter; how to write and structure reports,
  memos, and briefings; BLUF; how to weigh evidence.
- **Don't assume they know:** how LLMs work; a structured way to prompt; which
  information may go into ChatGPT under their agency's rules.
- **What they want:** faster, better first drafts of routine work, especially
  agendas, checklists, and email replies.
- **Likely worries:** *Am I allowed? Will it make things up in my analysis? Is
  this worth the time?* As analysts, they'll be quick to spot weak evidence.
  Use that: the Hallucination Hunt is built for them.

Pitch it as you would to colleagues: no basic definitions of research or
writing, no talking down, and real reasons rather than rules of thumb.

## 6. Scope

**In:** how ChatGPT generates text; writing, refining, and reusing prompts for
agendas, checklists, and email; checking outputs, especially numbers, sources,
and claims of cause; data-handling basics; accountability.

**Out, and where to send people who ask:**

| Topic | Send them to |
| --- | --- |
| Deep research features, data analysis with uploaded files, coding | A follow-on course, if one exists [TODO] |
| Building, buying, or deploying AI systems | The agency's Chief AI Officer or CIO office |
| AI governance and risk management | The agency's AI governance body |
| Classified environments | The agency's security office |
| Whether a specific real task is allowed | Their supervisor and agency AI policy |

# Take-home practice: ten prompts for the week after

One or two a day for a week, about ten minutes each. They keep the skills from
fading before they become habits.

**Use only invented or public information.** You're practising in the public
version of ChatGPT. For real work, use only the tools and information your agency
allows.

1. **Plain-language summary.** Take a press release from your agency's public
   website. "Summarise this in 100 words for a member of the public. Use only
   the text below; put [CHECK] if anything is unclear." Check every fact against
   the release.

2. **Ask it to ask you.** "I need an agenda for a project kickoff. Before you
   write anything, ask me five questions you'd need answered." Answer with
   invented details, then let it draft.

3. **Traceable findings.** Take the executive summary of a public report (GAO,
   CRS, or your agency's own). "List the three most important findings. After
   each, quote the sentence it came from." Check the quotes.

4. **Test your prompt card.** Run your card on all three inputs from a
   [test pack](block-6.md). Change one thing and re-run.

5. **Write your own chaos.** Write a confusing (invented) email with four buried
   questions. Run your email-reply prompt on it, then the coverage check (T4.1).
   Did it find all four?

6. **Red-team a public plan.** Paste a section of a published agency plan.
   "You are a sceptical reviewer. List the five biggest risks to delivering this,
   most serious first." Which critiques would you accept?

7. **Minutes to action items.** Find the minutes of a public federal advisory
   committee meeting. "Turn these into an action-item table: Action, Owner, Due
   date. [CHECK] where missing." Compare against the minutes.

8. **Same prompt twice.** Run any prompt from this week in two new chats. Which
   differences would matter at work?

9. **Prompt Golf.** Get a 30-minute agenda that meets five criteria you write
   first, in the fewest words of prompt. Which words did the work?

10. **Teach it back.** Explain the five parts and the missing-information test to
    a colleague in two minutes. Teaching is the best test of whether you've got it.

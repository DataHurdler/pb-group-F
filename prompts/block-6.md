# Block 6 prompts: Test packs for your prompt card

Use these to test your prompt card. Pick the pack nearest your task, or write
your own three inputs in the same pattern. All content is invented.

**Your test log** (on your prompt card): for each input, did it pass your
criteria? If not, what failed, and what one thing did you change?

Run at least the **normal** and **missing-information** inputs. The missing-
information test is the one most prompts fail.

## Writing your own pack

If your task isn't covered, write three short invented inputs:

1. **Normal**: a typical, well-organised example.
2. **Messy**: the same content, written the way people actually write it.
3. **Missing information**: one line that leaves out what matters most.

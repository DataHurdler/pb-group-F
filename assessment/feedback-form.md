## Reading the results

- Question 2 against the pre/post check: are learners confident about what they
  actually improved on?
- **Question 5 is a safety check.** If many say "no" or "not sure," the policy
  link wasn't clear enough. Fix Session 00 and the safe-use card.
- If the same session appears in both 6 and 7, it works for some learners and not
  others. Look at who.
